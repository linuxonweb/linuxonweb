Sampled textures through public Vulkan

Isolated static built-in driver integration (packet A4). SAMPLED images filled by vkCmdCopyBufferToImage, VkSampler nearest/linear with repeat/clamp/mirror addressing and COMBINED_IMAGE_SAMPLER descriptors read by application SPIR-V (fragment and vertex stages) on WebGPU or WebGL2. Twelve 16×16 readback frames plus negative cases. Bounded, nonconformant; no OpenGL, Firefox acceleration, decoder or physical GPU claim.
